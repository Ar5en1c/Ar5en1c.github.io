The Open-Weight
Race

November 2022  →  October 2026

After ChatGPT, the best AI lived behind a paywall on someone else's servers. Then a model leaked, a hobbyist got it running on a laptop, and a race started over who gets to download the brain. This timeline follows that one race.

First, the words

What "open" means here

A trained model is mostly a giant file of numbers called weights. Whoever holds that file can run the model, change it, and build on it. The race is about who gives that file away.

🔒
Closed

Rent it

You send text to the company's servers and get an answer back. You never see the weights. The company decides the price, the rules and when the model changes.

GPT-4, GPT-5, Claude, Gemini
⬇
Open weights

Download it

The weights are posted for anyone to download. You can run the model on your own computer, fine-tune it, or ship it in a product, within the terms of its license.

Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek, gpt-oss
📖
Fully open source

Rebuild it

Weights plus the training data details and code, so anyone could rebuild the model from scratch. The Open Source Initiative wrote this standard down in October 2024. Most "open" models don't meet it.

AI2's OLMo, EleutherAI's Pythia
The scoreboard

The gap is closing

Each arrow joins a closed model to the first open model that roughly matched it on the benchmarks the labs reported. The time it took open models to catch up went from more than a year to about four months.

Pairs are approximate and based on each lab's reported benchmark results. Epoch AI estimated in 2024 that the best open models trailed the best closed ones by about a year.

The players

Who is racing

The open side started as an American story with Meta out front. By 2025, Chinese labs were releasing many of the strongest open models, and in 2026 they released most of them.

Why this race matters

What's at stake

Cost

Free weights put a ceiling on what closed labs can charge. DeepSeek's low prices in 2024 set off a price war among Chinese AI companies.

Privacy and control

A model on your own laptop or phone doesn't send your data anywhere. Hospitals, banks and governments care about that a lot.

Safety

Once weights are out, anyone can strip the safety training. The US government studied this in 2024 and chose to watch closely instead of restricting open models.

Geopolitics

Open models spread a country's technology around the world. The US AI Action Plan in 2025 called for more American open models for that reason.

In 2023 the question was whether open models could keep up at all.
By October 2026 the best open model sits a few months behind the best closed one, and the question is how long any closed lead lasts.
